Household Finance App

Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 July 2026

This is a private, personal finance application used solely by the two members of our household to view and manage our own bank accounts in one place. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to, or used by, anyone else.

1 What data we access

The app accesses our own banking information — such as account balances and transaction history — through Enable Banking's regulated open banking API. Data is only ever accessed after the relevant account holder has given their explicit consent through their bank's secure authentication.

2 How the data is stored

  • All financial data is stored only on our own devices and/or our own private server.
  • It is not uploaded to any third-party cloud storage beyond what is technically required to connect to our banks.
  • We do not sell, rent, monetise, or otherwise share the data with any third party.

3 Third parties

The only external party involved is Enable Banking, the regulated technical provider that securely connects the app to our banks under open banking rules. No financial data is shared with anyone for advertising, profiling, analytics, or resale.

4 Consent and revocation

Access to each account is granted only with the account holder's explicit consent and can be revoked at any time — either through the bank (for example, in its app or online banking consent settings) or by removing the connection within this app. Once consent is revoked, the app stops accessing new data from that account.

5 Data retention

Data is kept only for as long as it is useful for managing our own household finances, and can be deleted at any time by removing it from our device or server.
